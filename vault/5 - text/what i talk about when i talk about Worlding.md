Titel Referenz: what i talk about when i talk about running - Haruki Murakami
	Murakamis book isn’t a training manual; it’s more like a quiet logbook of how running structures his life and creativity. Murakami writes about starting to run in his 30s, training for marathons and an ultramarathon, and how the discipline, solitude, and repetitive effort of running mirror the discipline of writing novels. A recurring theme is accepting your own limits: he doesn’t pose as a hero, just as someone steadily putting in the miles and thinking about aging, pain, and what it means to keep going.
